## Verdict

**overstated**

'Remain unreached' matches: the known isotopes are far from N = 184. 'Non-trivial 1989 prediction' is overstated. Long-lived elements near Z = 114 had been predicted in print since 1966, twenty-three years before the 1989 broadcast, and the systematic name for element 115 dates from 1979 (`chatt1979`). Saying in 1989 that element 115 could exist repeated a published expectation. The unusual part of the claim was a stable isotope in weighable amounts, and that part is contradicted by every measurement. Note also 'long-lived' in the predictions means minutes to millions of years for nuclei near N = 184, not stable, and not specifically element 115.
