**Source.** Öhrström, Lars; Reedijk, Jan. Names and symbols of the elements with atomic numbers 113, 115, 117 and 118 (IUPAC Recommendations 2016). Pure and Applied Chemistry 88(12) : 1225-1229.
DOI [10.1515/pac-2016-0501](https://doi.org/10.1515/pac-2016-0501).

**What was read.** abstract (Crossref deposit) and the IUPAC statements of 8 June and 28 November 2016 (full text).

**Design and sample.** IUPAC naming recommendation. Not a study.

## What the source says

- "moscovium with the symbol Mc, for the element with Z=115" (abstract).
- IUPAC, 28 November 2016: "On 28 November 2016, the International Union of Pure and Applied Chemistry (IUPAC) approved the name and symbols for four elements".
- IUPAC, 8 June 2016: "Moscovium is in recognition of the Moscow region and honors the ancient Russian land that is the home of the Joint Institute for Nuclear Research".
