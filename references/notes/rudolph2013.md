# rudolph2013 — Rudolph (2013)

*Reading note, 20 Nov 2026. Up: [references](../README.md). Record: `references/sources.json`, id `rudolph2013`; graph node `ref-rudolph-2013-spectroscopy-element-115`.*

**Source.** Rudolph, D.; Forsberg, U.; Golubev, P.; Sarmiento, L. G.; et al. (51 authors, Lund University and GSI). Spectroscopy of Element 115 Decay Chains. Physical Review Letters 111 : 112502.
DOI [10.1103/PhysRevLett.111.112502](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.111.112502). PMID 24074079.

**What was read.** abstract only (INSPIRE-HEP record of the DOI); the paper is paywalled.

**Design and sample.** Independent experiment at GSI Darmstadt (TASCA separator) led by Lund University: 48Ca on 243Am with α, X-ray and γ coincidence spectroscopy. Sample: thirty correlated α-decay chains.

## What the source says

- "Thirty correlated α-decay chains were detected following the fusion-evaporation reaction 48Ca+243Am." (abstract).
- "The observations are consistent with previous assignments of similar decay chains to originate from element Z=115." (abstract).

## Verdict

**matches**

An independent laboratory saw the same chains. The paper's own verb is 'consistent with', which is weaker than 'confirms' but the same in substance. Crossref dates the article 2013 (Phys. Rev. Lett. 111, 112502); 'August 2013' is the month of the announcement and is not in the record: NOT FOUND. A later Berkeley experiment (`gates2015`) says X-rays did not allow 'a definitive Z identification', so 'confirmed by X-ray fingerprint' should not be written.
