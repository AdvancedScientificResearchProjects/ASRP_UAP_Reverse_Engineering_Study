- "Fifty-five new decay chains of 288Mc and six chains assigned to 289Mc were detected." (abstract).
- A new isotope, 264Lr, "with a half-life of 4.9 (+2.1, −1.3) h was identified" (abstract).

## Verdict

**not checked (no full text)**

The repository's number is what the interviewee said in 2021. The published paper reports 61 chains (55 + 6) for the first experiment. The two are of the same order, but 'about 70 in 40 days' is testimony and the '40 days' could not be checked in the abstract. Where a number is printed, use the published one and say which run it is.
