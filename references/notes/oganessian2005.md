**Source.** Oganessian, Yu. Ts.; Utyonkov, V. K.; Dmitriev, S. N.; Lobanov, Yu. V.; et al. (37 authors). Synthesis of elements 115 and 113 in the reaction 243Am+48Ca. Physical Review C 72 : 034611.
DOI [10.1103/PhysRevC.72.034611](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevC.72.034611).

**What was read.** abstract only (INSPIRE-HEP record of the DOI); the paper is paywalled.

**Design and sample.** Full report of the two 2003 experiments plus an independent chemistry experiment. Sample: the same four chains; 15 spontaneous-fission events of 268Db in the chemistry experiment.

## What the source says

- "Two new elements with atomic numbers 113 and 115 were observed for the first time." (abstract).
- "a long-lived spontaneous fission activity, 268Db (15 events), was found to be chemically consistent with the fifth group of the periodic table" (abstract).
- "The odd-odd isotope 288-115 was observed with largest cross section of about 4 pb." (abstract).
