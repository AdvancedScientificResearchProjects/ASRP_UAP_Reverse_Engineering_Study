## What the source says

- "The new isotope 286Mc was synthesized, and its half-life of 20 (+98, −9) ms and α-particle energy of 10.71 ± 0.02 MeV were determined." (abstract).
- "The spontaneous fission of 279Rg was observed for the first time in one of four new decay chains of 287Mc." (abstract).
