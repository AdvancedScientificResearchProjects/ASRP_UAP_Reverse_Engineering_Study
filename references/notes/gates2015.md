**Source.** Gates, J. M.; Gregorich, K. E.; Gothe, O. R.; Uribe, E. C.; et al. (33 authors). Decay spectroscopy of element 115 daughters: 280Rg -> 276Mt and 276Mt -> 272Bh. Physical Review C 92 : 021301(R).
DOI [10.1103/PhysRevC.92.021301](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevC.92.021301).

**What was read.** abstract only (INSPIRE-HEP record of the DOI); the paper is paywalled.

**Design and sample.** Berkeley 88-inch cyclotron, same reaction, α-photon and α-X-ray spectroscopy. Sample: 46 decay chains, combined with 22 from a similar experiment.

## What the source says

- "Forty-six decay chains, assigned to the decay of 288-115, were produced" (abstract).
- "Compton scattering of higher-energy γ rays and discrete transitions are present in the K x-ray region preventing a definitive Z identification to be made based on observation of characteristic K x-ray energies." (abstract).
