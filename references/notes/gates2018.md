# gates2018 — Gates (2018)

*Reading note, 20 Nov 2026. Up: [references](../README.md). Record: `references/sources.json`, id `gates2018`; graph node `ref-gates-2018-first-direct-measurements`.*

**Source.** Gates, J. M.; Pang, G. K.; Pore, J. L.; Gregorich, K. E.; et al. (22 authors). First Direct Measurements of Superheavy-Element Mass Numbers. Physical Review Letters 121 : 222501.
DOI [10.1103/PhysRevLett.121.222501](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.121.222501).

**What was read.** full text (accepted manuscript LLNL-JRNL-754504 at OSTI, id 1492331).

**Design and sample.** Berkeley 88-inch cyclotron with the BGS separator and the FIONA mass analyser. Sample: two α-decay chains (one event at A/q = 284, one at A/q = 288).

## What the source says

- "One event occurred at A/q=284 and was assigned to 284Nh (Z=113), the α-decay daughter of 288Mc (Z=115), while the second occurred at A/q=288 and was assigned to 288Mc." (abstract, manuscript p. 1).
- "This experiment represents the first direct measurements of the mass numbers of superheavy elements, confirming previous (indirect) mass-number assignments." (abstract, manuscript p. 1).
