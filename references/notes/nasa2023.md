**What was read.** full text (36 pages).

**Design and sample.** Report of a 16-member independent study team convened by NASA. A review of data sources and methods; no new measurement.

## What the source says

- "At present, analysis of UAP data is hampered by poor sensor calibration, the lack of multiple measurements, the lack of sensor metadata, and the lack of baseline data." (Executive Summary, PDF p. 5).
- Eyewitness reports "on their own can be interesting and compelling, but are not reproducible and usually lack the information needed to make any definitive conclusions about a UAP's provenance." (PDF p. 11).
- "At this point there is no reason to conclude that existing UAP reports have an extraterrestrial source." (PDF p. 35).
