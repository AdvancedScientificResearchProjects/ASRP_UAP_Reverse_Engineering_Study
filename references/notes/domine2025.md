**Source.** Domine, Laura; Biswas, Ankit; Cloete, Richard; Delacroix, Alex; et al. (16 authors). Commissioning an All-Sky Infrared Camera Array for Detection of Airborne Objects. Sensors 25(3) : 783.
DOI [10.3390/s25030783](https://doi.org/10.3390/s25030783). PMID 39943425.

**What was read.** full text (Europe PMC open XML, PMC11820869).

**Design and sample.** Commissioning of an array of eight uncooled long-wave infrared cameras, five months of field operation. Sample: about 500,000 reconstructed trajectories.

## What the source says

- "Approximately 500,000 trajectories of various aerial objects are reconstructed from this five-month commissioning period." (abstract).
- "About 16% of the trajectories are flagged as outliers and manually examined in the IR images. From these ∼80,000 outliers and 144 trajectories remain ambiguous, which are likely mundane objects but cannot be further elucidated at this stage" (abstract).
- Acceptance "of 41% for ADS-B-equipped aircraft, and a mean frame-by-frame aircraft detection efficiency ... of 36%" (abstract).
- "an upper limit of 18,271 outliers for the five-month interval at a 95% confidence level" (abstract).
