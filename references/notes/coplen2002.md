# coplen2002 — Coplen (2002)

*Reading note, 20 Nov 2026. Up: [references](../README.md). Record: `references/sources.json`, id `coplen2002`; graph node `ref-coplen-2002-compilation-minimum-maximum`.*

**Source.** Coplen, T. B.; Hopple, J. A.; Böhlke, J. K.; Peiser, H. S.; Rieder, S. E.; et al.. Compilation of minimum and maximum isotope ratios of selected elements in naturally occurring terrestrial materials and reagents. U.S. Geological Survey Water-Resources Investigations Report 01-4222 : 98 p..
DOI [10.3133/wri014222](https://doi.org/10.3133/wri014222).

**What was read.** full text (USGS PDF); only the purpose statement and the magnesium section were read.

**Design and sample.** Compilation of published extreme isotope ratios in natural terrestrial materials and reagents for 20 elements. Not an experiment.

## What the source says

- "The purpose of this report is to compile ranges of isotope abundance variations and corresponding atomic weights in selected materials containing 20 chemical elements (H, Li, B, C, N, O, Mg, Si, S, Cl, Ca, Cr, Fe, Cu, Zn, Se, Mo, Pd, Te, and Tl) from published data." (PDF p. 14).
- Magnesium ratios "are measured on terrestrial materials and reported as δ26Mg values relative to NIST SRM 980 magnesium metal, which is assigned a δ26Mg value of 0 ‰." (PDF p. 56).
- CIAAW page for magnesium (read 20 Nov 2026): abundance intervals "24Mg [0.7888, 0.7905]; 25Mg [0.099 88, 0.100 34]; 26Mg [0.1096, 0.1109]".
