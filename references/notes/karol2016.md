# karol2016 — Karol (2016)

*Reading note, 20 Nov 2026. Up: [references](../README.md). Record: `references/sources.json`, id `karol2016`; graph node `ref-karol-2016-discovery-elements-atomic`.*

**Source.** Karol, Paul J.; Barber, Robert C.; Sherrill, Bradley M.; Vardaci, Emanuele; Yamazaki, Toshimitsu. Discovery of the elements with atomic numbers Z = 113, 115 and 117 (IUPAC Technical Report). Pure and Applied Chemistry 88(1-2) : 139-153.
DOI [10.1515/pac-2015-0502](https://doi.org/10.1515/pac-2015-0502).

**What was read.** abstract (Crossref deposit) and the IUPAC statement of 30 Dec 2015 (full text); the report itself was not read (browser check on the publisher site).

**Design and sample.** Review by the fourth IUPAC/IUPAP Joint Working Party of the claims for elements 113, 115, 117 and 118. Not an experiment.

## What the source says

- "it was determined that the RIKEN collaboration has fulfilled those Criteria for element Z=113. The Dubna–Livermore–Oak Ridge collaborations claims for 115 and 117 are also in compliance." (abstract).
- IUPAC statement of 30 December 2015: "The collaboration between the Joint Institute for Nuclear Research in Dubna, Russia; Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory, California, USA; and Oak Ridge National Laboratory, Oak Ridge, Tennessee, USA have fulfilled the criteria for element Z=115, 117".
- Neither text mentions GSI, Lund or Berkeley.

## What the ASRP text says about it

- `dubna-element-115-analysis/analysis/jinr-facts-and-history.md:93` — "The IUPAC/IUPAP Joint Working Party initially declined to recognise the 2004 discovery in 2011, then accepted it following the August 2013 GSI Darmstadt / Lund University independent replication."
- `dubna-element-115-analysis/analysis/lazar-dubna-connection.md:83` — "formal recognition came after the August 2013 GSI + Lund independent replication".

## Verdict

**overstated**

Recognition did come after 2013 in time. But 'accepted it following the ... replication' reads as cause, and the two IUPAC texts read here credit only the Dubna–Livermore–Oak Ridge collaboration and give no such reason. The Lund group itself later published a paper questioning part of the evidence (`forsberg2016`, title only). Reword to: 'recognised by IUPAC and IUPAP on 30 December 2015, priority to JINR, LLNL and ORNL'. The body of the report must be read before any sentence about why it was accepted.
