Bibliographic details agree. Everything the card says about the content is unchecked, and the card itself says it rests on the title. Two cautions that need no full text. First, a method paper cannot 'test' a claim about element 115: isotope-ratio mass spectrometry of a stone or a metal measures stable isotopes of ordinary elements, and no moscovium isotope lives longer than about a second (`nubase2020`). Second, the fragment in this study is described by the study's own reports as probably a fossil; the campaign text is right to plan mineral and X-ray methods first.
