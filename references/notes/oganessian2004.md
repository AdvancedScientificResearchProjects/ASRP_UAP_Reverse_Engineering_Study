# oganessian2004 — Oganessian (2004)

*Reading note, 20 Nov 2026. Up: [references](../README.md). Record: `references/sources.json`, id `oganessian2004`; graph node `ref-oganessian-2004-experiments-synthesis-element`.*

**Source.** Oganessian, Yu. Ts.; Utyonkov, V. K.; Lobanov, Yu. V.; Abdullin, F. Sh.; Polyakov, A. N.; et al. (26 authors, JINR Dubna and LLNL Livermore). Experiments on the synthesis of element 115 in the reaction 243Am(48Ca,xn)291-x115. Physical Review C 69 : 021601(R).
DOI [10.1103/PhysRevC.69.021601](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevC.69.021601).

**What was read.** abstract only (INSPIRE-HEP record of the DOI); the paper is paywalled.

**Design and sample.** Heavy-ion fusion experiment at the U400 cyclotron with the Dubna gas-filled recoil separator: 48Ca beam on a 243Am target, two beam energies (248 and 253 MeV), a beam dose of 4.3×10^18 at the first. Sample: four decay chains, that is four atoms.

## What the source says

- "we observed three similar decay chains consisting of five consecutive α decays, all detected in time intervals of about 20 s and terminated at a later time by a spontaneous fission" (abstract).
- "we registered a different decay chain of four consecutive α decays detected in a time interval of about 0.5 s" (abstract).
- The chains are assigned to "the parent isotopes of the new element 115, 288-115 and 287-115, produced in the 3n- and 4n-evaporation channels with cross sections of about 3 pb and 1 pb" (abstract).
- Half-lives of the individual nuclei are in the body of the paper, not in the abstract: NOT FOUND here. Evaluated values are in the note `nubase2020`.
