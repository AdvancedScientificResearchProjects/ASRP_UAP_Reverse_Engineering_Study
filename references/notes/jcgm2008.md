**Source.** Joint Committee for Guides in Metrology (JCGM). Evaluation of measurement data - Guide to the expression of uncertainty in measurement (JCGM 100:2008, GUM 1995 with minor corrections). BIPM / JCGM guide.
Official address: <https://www.bipm.org/documents/20126/2071204/JCGM_100_2008_E.pdf>.

**What was read.** full text; clause 3.1.2 read.

**Design and sample.** International guide to stating measurement uncertainty. Not a study.

## What the source says

- "the result of a measurement is only an approximation or estimate of the value of the measurand and thus is complete only when accompanied by a statement of the uncertainty of that estimate." (clause 3.1.2, PDF p. 16).
