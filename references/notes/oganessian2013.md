# oganessian2013 — Oganessian (2013)

*Reading note, 20 Nov 2026. Up: [references](../README.md). Record: `references/sources.json`, id `oganessian2013`; graph node `ref-oganessian-2013-investigation-243am-48ca`.*

**Source.** Oganessian, Yu. Ts.; Abdullin, F. Sh.; Dmitriev, S. N.; Gostic, J. M.; et al. (23 authors). Investigation of the 243Am+48Ca reaction products previously observed in the experiments on elements 113, 115, and 117. Physical Review C 87 : 014302.
DOI [10.1103/PhysRevC.87.014302](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevC.87.014302).

**What was read.** abstract only (INSPIRE-HEP record of the DOI); the paper is paywalled.

**Design and sample.** Long Dubna run, 1 November 2010 to 26 February 2012, total beam dose 3.3×10^19. Sample: 28 chains of 288-115, four events of 289-115, one chain of 287-115.

## What the source says

- "The 28 detected decay chains of 288-115 show that this isotope is produced with the maximum probability at E*=34.0–38.3 MeV" (abstract).
- The results "reproduce in full the results of the first experiment of 2003 that reported the discovery of elements 115 and 113" (abstract).
- "observation of the isotope 289-115 in cross-bombardment reactions with the targets of 243Am and 249Bk provide additional evidence of the identification" (abstract).
